Species Sound Classifier

Upload a field recording and an EfficientNet-B0 model identifies which of 206 species (birds, amphibians, insects and mammals from the BirdCLEF+ 2025 dataset) it hears.

The model only knows the 206 species of the BirdCLEF+ 2025 competition (set in Colombia), so any recording gets one of them as an answer; it was trained on single-species recordings and is less reliable on noisy soundscapes.
